# Shyam Kirana Pasal — Shop Rules and Policies

## Credit limit
Each regular customer can take goods on credit up to a maximum of Rs 2000 in total.
New customers (less than 3 months) have a lower credit limit of Rs 500.
If a customer is over their limit, they must pay part of the balance before taking more credit.

## Payment collection days
Credit is collected every Sunday and on the 1st of every month.
Customers who have not paid anything for 30 days get a polite reminder call.
Balances older than 60 days are not given any new credit until they are cleared.

## Discounts
Customers who clear their full balance on time get 2% off their next purchase.
Bulk rice purchases of 25 kg or more get Rs 100 off.
There is no discount on items bought on credit.

## Items not given on credit
Cigarettes, alcohol, mobile recharge cards, and gas cylinders are cash only.
Credit is allowed for rice, lentils (dal), oil, sugar, tea, spices, soap, and other daily groceries.

## Opening hours
The shop is open from 6:30 AM to 9:00 PM every day.
On Saturday the shop closes at 1:00 PM.
The shop is closed on Dashain Tika day and Laxmi Puja day.

## Returns
Packed items can be returned within 3 days with the bill if unopened.
Vegetables, milk, and loose items cannot be returned.
